Order Management System

Sowing Log & Production Map

Little River Hydroponics — VIC
● Live

Digital sowing register that goes beyond simple logging. Organises records by variety and line, shows current available stock per product, and generates a visual production map showing what is growing in each system and line. Fertiliser Consumption Monitor

Little River Hydroponics — VIC
● Live

Daily tracking of fertiliser consumption per growing system. Records usage volumes, acid refills, EC and pH monitoring, and probe calibrations. Enables deviation detection correlated with environmental variables such as temperature, rainfall, and time of reading. Complemented by digital formats for machinery checks and incident recording.

Daily usage log EC & pH tracking Deviation alerts Machinery checklists

Worker Time Tracking System

Little River Hydroponics — VIC
● Live

Full digital time-keeping system built on Google Forms and Google Sheets. Workers log in and out via published web forms with PIN authentication. The system automatically calculates raw hours, deducts breaks, and generates a weekly summary filterable by worker and date range. Includes manual record support, employee register, SOPs, and payroll preparation output. 779+ entries recorded to date.

Log In / Log Out forms Auto hour calculation Weekly summary PIN authentication 779+ records
